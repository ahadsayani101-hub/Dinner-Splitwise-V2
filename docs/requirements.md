# Client requirements

## Confirmed
- **Itemized split:** each person pays for what they ordered. The client said an even split doesn't feel fair.
- **Tax and tip:** split proportionally to each person's share of the item subtotal.
- **Receipt capture:** the client wants a **video** of the receipt, not a photo. *(On hold, see open questions.)*

### Example
Subtotal $60, tax $5.40, tip $12.
| Person | Items | Share | Tax | Tip | Pays |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Alex | $30 | 50% | $2.70 | $6.00 | $38.70 |
| Sam | $18 | 30% | $1.62 | $3.60 | $23.22 |
| Jo | $12 | 20% | $1.08 | $2.40 | $15.48 |
| **Total** | $60 | | $5.40 | $12.00 | **$77.40** |
